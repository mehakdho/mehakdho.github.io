The partners stay in your network
  1. 1

    Discovery Phase 01

    ICP definition, offer review, target market sizing. Buying signals defined and dated: hiring, expansion, regulatory deadlines. Deliverable: target account specification.

  2. 2

    Account mapping Phase 02

    Relationship mapping per target account: referral partners, suppliers, advisers. Introduction route selected per account. Deliverable: account map with warm-introduction paths.

  3. 3

    Contact validation Phase 03

    One decision-maker per account, signing authority confirmed. Direct line verified to the named person; switchboards and shared lines excluded. Deliverable: validated contact list.

  4. 4

    Sequence build Phase 04

    Multi-step sequence: problem statement, one-page brief, meeting request with two fixed times. Each step triggered by a reply. Deliverable: approved sequence and copy.

  5. 5

    Launch and handover Phase 05

    Go-live, calendar integration, meeting briefs per booking. Referral partner relationships documented and transferred. Deliverable: live pipeline and partner register.
