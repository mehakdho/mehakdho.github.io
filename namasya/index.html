Signal 1 · they bought before
  1. Registered sale records around Vadodara: who bought, in which society, when.
  2. Sunfin's own past clients and enquiries, the strongest list of all.

Signal 2 · they are looking now
  1. They enquired on another project or portal in the last 90 days.
  2. They clicked a Shree Namasya ad, opened the brochure or replied on WhatsApp.
  3. Our callers ring them, confirm budget, timing and loan, and book the site visit.

Shree Namasya · inbound team

Every enquiry answered

  1. Camtech's Meta ads keep running. The AI calls every enquiry they bring in within a minute, in Gujarati, Hindi or English.
  2. It asks what your team asks: budget, loan, when they want to move, who decides.
  3. Ready buyers get a site visit booked on the call. Your team gets a short note before the site visit.
  4. Baroda buyers come 3 to 5 times with family before booking. The AI books each return site visit and reminds them.
  5. The not-ready ones get WhatsApp follow-ups and a call back in 30, 60 or 90 days, without anyone tracking it.
Your callers spend their day with buyers who already said yes to a site visit.
Blue Sun · cold-calling team

Our callers ring a new list every day

  1. Every week we build the cold-calling list. First: families who already bought around Vadodara and are buying again. Then enquiries.
  2. Each number comes with the reason: bought before, searching now, works nearby.
  3. Each morning our callers get the day's numbers, most likely first, with what to say.
  4. The same system runs for every project Sunfin sells, each with its own ads and script.
Your team only meets buyers who said yes to a site visit.
