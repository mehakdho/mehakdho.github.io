    Buyers with proof they buy Buying behaviour

    We only approach buyers who show they actually buy: recent acquisitions in your sector, or a fund raised and not yet spent. Brokers and advisers are left out.

  2. 2

    The person who can make an offer Quality

    One decision-maker per buyer: the partner who can put an offer on the table, not an analyst.

  3. 3

    The warm way in Warm introduction

    Buyers listen to the people around their deals: operating partners, the CEOs of companies they own, the lenders they use. We reach them through those people.

    Buyers see the opportunity, not your name. Your name is shared only with the buyers you approve.
