How it works

You stay in control at every step.

STEP 1

Your business, your terms

What you want from the sale: price, timing, who you would and would not sell to, and what happens to your team.

STEP 2

The buyer list

Acquirers whose current plans fit your company, checked for real buying activity before any contact. You approve the list.

STEP 3

Confidential outreach

Each buyer's decision-maker hears about the opportunity without your name. Interested buyers are qualified first.

STEP 4

Buyers on your calendar

Serious buyers are booked in the same window with notes on each, so you compare offers side by side.

We bring qualified buyers to the table. We do not value your business, give legal or tax advice, or negotiate on your behalf; your own advisers handle the transaction. No sale price is guaranteed.
